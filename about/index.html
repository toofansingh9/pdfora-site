About PDFora

PDFora is a free set of PDF tools that runs in your web browser. It was built around one idea: most everyday PDF jobs — merging, splitting, compressing, rotating, signing — don't need your documents to be uploaded to somebody's server. Modern browsers are powerful enough to do the work themselves.

That's how PDFora is different. For the vast majority of our 47 tools, your file is opened and processed on your own device. It never travels over the internet, we never see it, and there is nothing for us to store, scan or delete. Close the tab and it's gone. For the handful of format conversions that genuinely need heavy software (PDF to Word, Excel and PowerPoint), we use a secure conversion pipeline over an encrypted connection, and files are deleted automatically after processing.

What you get

  • 47 tools covering organizing, editing, signing, converting, compressing and automating PDFs.
  • Free, without an account. No signup, no daily limits, no watermarks stamped on your documents.
  • Privacy by design. Browser-local processing wherever technically possible.
  • 29 languages. The whole site works in English, हिन्दी, Español, Français, Deutsch, 日本語, العربية and more.

How the tools are built

The browser-based tools are built on well-known open-source PDF libraries — pdf-lib for creating and editing documents, Mozilla's PDF.js for rendering pages, Tesseract.js for OCR and JSZip for multi-file downloads. They load in your browser only when you pick a file. Office conversions (PDF to and from Word, Excel and PowerPoint, and HTML to PDF) are handled by CloudConvert, as described in our Privacy Policy. Each tool page says which of the two it uses.

How we keep it free

PDFora is supported by advertising and an optional Pro plan for heavy conversion use. The browser-based tools are free and will stay free — they run on your hardware, so our costs stay small and we'd rather earn your repeat visits than your credit card number.
