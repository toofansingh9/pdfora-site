Most forms start life as a static document: a Word export, a scan, a nicely designed PDF that people are somehow expected to fill in. They print it, write on it, scan it back — or type over it awkwardly in an editor. This tool ends that cycle. It lets you create a fillable PDF form by placing real interactive text fields and checkboxes anywhere on your existing document.

The workflow is direct. Your PDF appears on screen; you click where an answer should go, and a field is placed there. Drag fields to fine-tune their position, resize them to fit the blank they sit over, add checkboxes next to the yes/no options. When you export, you get a genuine AcroForm PDF — the standard interactive format that Adobe Acrobat, browsers and mobile readers all know how to fill.

The document you are converting never leaves your computer, because everything runs locally in your browser. There is no signup, no watermark stamped on your form, no charge, and no limit on how many forms you build.

How to create a fillable PDF form online

  1. Open the PDF you want to make fillable — an application, contract or questionnaire. It loads locally in your browser.
  2. Click on the page wherever a typed answer belongs; a text field appears at that spot.
  3. Add checkboxes beside any yes/no items or multiple-choice options by clicking in place.
  4. Drag each field to line it up with the printed blank, and resize it so longer answers have room.
  5. Repeat across every page until each question has a field.
  6. Export the file — you get a real fillable AcroForm PDF ready to send, which recipients complete in any standard PDF reader.

When to use this tool

  • Turn a rental application that tenants used to print and scan into a form they can complete on their phone.
  • Make a club membership or event registration sheet fillable so responses come back typed instead of handwritten.
  • Convert a patient intake questionnaire into an interactive form the clinic can email ahead of appointments.
  • Add fields to a freelance contract template so each new client's name, dates and rate can be typed in cleanly.
  • Rebuild an old scanned permission slip as a fillable PDF that parents return by email.
  • Give an internal expense claim or leave request document proper fields and checkboxes instead of relying on people editing the file.

Tips for the best results

  • Size text fields generously — an email address needs more width than you think, and cramped fields frustrate people.
  • Line fields up with the printed blanks and labels on the page; the underlying document provides the captions, your fields provide the input.
  • Use checkboxes for anything with fixed options rather than expecting people to type an X into a text field.
  • Fill the exported form yourself once before sending it out — a two-minute test catches misplaced or missing fields.
  • Keep the exported fillable version as your master template, and consider flattening completed copies later so responses cannot be changed.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AcroForm?

AcroForm is the standard format for interactive PDF forms, supported by virtually every PDF reader — Acrobat, browser viewers, Preview, mobile apps. This tool exports genuine AcroForm fields, not a lookalike overlay, so your form is fillable and saveable anywhere.

Do I need an existing PDF to start from?

Yes — this tool adds fields on top of a document you already have, such as a designed form, contract or scan. Design the layout in any app, export to PDF, then place the fields here.

Can I adjust a field after placing it?

Yes. Every field can be dragged to a new position and resized by its edges until it sits exactly over the blank line or box it belongs to. A misplaced field is a two-second fix, not a restart.

What field types can I add?

Text fields for typed answers and checkboxes for options — the two building blocks that cover most real forms: applications, intake sheets, contracts, registrations and sign-offs.

Can recipients save their filled-in answers?

Yes. Because the export is a standard fillable PDF form, recipients open it in any modern reader, type their answers, tick the boxes and save or return the file with the data preserved.

Is the document I upload kept private?

There is no upload at all — the PDF is processed entirely in your browser and never touches a server. Combined with no signup, no watermarks and no usage limits, you can build as many fillable forms as you need at no cost.
