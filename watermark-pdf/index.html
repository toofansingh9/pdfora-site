Add a watermark to PDF files without installing anything or handing your documents to a stranger's server. PDFora stamps your custom text — DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL, your company name, a client reference — across every page of the document. The whole process runs inside your browser using local processing, so the file never leaves your computer. That matters when the PDF you are marking is exactly the kind of document you would not want floating around.

You control how the watermark looks. Type any text you like, adjust the font size to suit the page, and set the opacity anywhere from a faint 0.1 ghost to a bold, fully opaque stamp. Flip on the 45-degree diagonal option and the text sweeps across the page the way classic DRAFT stamps do. The mark is applied consistently to every page, so a 200-page contract takes the same few seconds as a two-page memo.

PDFora is free, with no signup and no file limits. Worth saying plainly: many watermark tools add their own branding to your output. We never do. The only watermark on your finished PDF is the one you chose to put there. Use it once or a hundred times a day — nothing is counted or throttled.

How to add a watermark to a PDF online

  1. Open the watermark tool at getpdfora.com and select your PDF, or drag the file straight onto the page.
  2. Type the watermark text you want stamped on every page — for example CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, or your business name.
  3. Set the font size so the mark is readable without burying the content underneath.
  4. Adjust the opacity slider between 0 and 1 — around 0.2 to 0.3 gives a subtle mark that leaves the text legible.
  5. Tick the diagonal option if you want the text angled at 45 degrees across the page, or leave it off for a horizontal stamp.
  6. Click to apply, then download your watermarked PDF. Everything happens locally in your browser.

When to use this tool

  • Stamp DRAFT across a contract before sending it to the other side, so nobody signs a version that was never final.
  • Mark internal reports CONFIDENTIAL before circulating them beyond your immediate team.
  • Add your studio or firm name to design proofs and proposals so they cannot be quietly passed off elsewhere.
  • Label sample chapters or preview copies of an ebook with NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION.
  • Tag invoices or statements with PAID or COPY so originals and duplicates never get confused.
  • Watermark student handouts or exam papers with a course code and term so old versions are easy to spot.

Tips for the best results

  • Keep opacity low — 0.15 to 0.3 — when the document still needs to be read closely; save 0.6 and above for pages meant to be unusable as copies.
  • The 45-degree diagonal covers more of the page with a single line of text, which makes the mark harder to crop out.
  • Short text in a large font size reads better than a long sentence in a small one; 'DRAFT — v3' beats a full disclaimer.
  • Watermark a copy, not your only original. The tool never touches your source file, but keeping a clean master is good habit anyway.
  • For legal or client documents, put the recipient's name in the watermark — leaked copies then identify themselves.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded when I add a watermark?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using local processing. The file is opened, stamped, and saved on your own machine, and nothing is sent to PDFora's servers.

Will PDFora add its own watermark to my file?

Never. Unlike many free tools, we do not brand your output. The only watermark on the finished PDF is the text you typed yourself.

Can I control how transparent the watermark is?

Yes. Opacity is adjustable from 0 to 1. A low value like 0.2 gives a faint background mark, while 1 produces solid, fully opaque text.

Does the watermark go on every page?

It does. Your text is stamped on each page of the document automatically, whether the file has two pages or several hundred.

Can I place the watermark diagonally?

Yes. There is an option to rotate the text 45 degrees, which gives the classic corner-to-corner DRAFT look. Leave it unticked for a horizontal stamp instead.

Is there a limit on file size or number of files?

There is no fixed cap and no daily quota — the tool is free and unlimited, with no signup. Because processing is local, very large files are limited only by your device's memory.
