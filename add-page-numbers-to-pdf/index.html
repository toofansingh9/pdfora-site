Add page numbers to PDF documents in seconds, right in your browser. A merged report, a scanned thesis, a contract assembled from three sources — documents like these often arrive with no numbering at all, and printing eighty loose sheets without it is asking for trouble. PDFora stamps clean, consistent numbers onto every page while the file stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded, because the entire tool runs on local processing.

You choose where the numbers sit and how they read. Positions cover bottom center, bottom right, bottom left, top center, and top right, so the numbering can stay clear of headers, footers, or binding margins. Formats include a plain '1', the fuller '1 of N', or 'Page 1' for a more formal look. If your document is a chapter that starts mid-book, set a custom starting number and the count picks up from there.

Like every PDFora tool, this one is free with no signup, no watermarks stamped on your output, and no limit on how many files you number. Court filings, dissertations, board packs — the numbering you get is the numbering you keep, with no logo in the corner.

How to add page numbers to a PDF online

  1. Go to the page numbers tool on getpdfora.com and drop in your PDF file.
  2. Pick a position: bottom center, bottom right, bottom left, top center, or top right.
  3. Choose a format — a bare number like '1', the total-aware '1 of N', or the labeled 'Page 1'.
  4. Set the starting number. Leave it at 1, or start at 47 if this file continues an earlier section.
  5. Apply the numbering and let your browser process the file locally — no upload, no waiting on a server.
  6. Download the numbered PDF and check the first and last pages before printing or sharing.

When to use this tool

  • Number a thesis or dissertation before submission, when the university requires every page to carry sequential numbering.
  • Prepare legal exhibits or court bundles where 'Page 12 of 240' style references keep everyone literally on the same page.
  • Fix a merged PDF stitched together from several reports, so the combined file reads as one continuous document.
  • Add numbers to scanned archives — old minutes, ledgers, correspondence — that never had them in the first place.
  • Number training manuals and employee handbooks so staff can cite 'page 34' instead of describing where something roughly is.
  • Continue numbering across split volumes by starting the second file at the number where the first one ended.

Tips for the best results

  • Use '1 of N' for anything printed and handed around — a missing sheet is obvious when the last page says 80 of 80.
  • If pages already have a footer, place numbers at the top center or top right to avoid overlapping text.
  • Choose bottom left or bottom right, not center, for documents that will be hole-punched and bound, keeping numbers away from the spine side.
  • When numbering a chapter extracted from a longer work, set the custom start number to match the original pagination so citations still line up.
  • Number the final version, after merging and deleting pages — renumbering is easy, but stale numbers on a reshuffled file cause confusion.

Frequently asked questions

Does my file get uploaded to a server?

No. The tool works entirely inside your browser with local processing. Your PDF stays on your computer from start to finish, which makes it safe for confidential contracts and unpublished work.

Can I start numbering from a number other than 1?

Yes. Enter any custom starting number and the count begins there. This is handy for chapters, appendices, or the second half of a document you split earlier.

What numbering formats are available?

Three: a plain number such as '1', the format '1 of N' which includes the total page count, and 'Page 1'. Pick whichever matches your document's style.

Where on the page can the numbers appear?

Five positions are supported: bottom center, bottom right, bottom left, top center, and top right. Choose one that stays clear of existing headers or footers.

Is the tool really free, and does it mark my file?

It is free with no signup and no usage cap, and PDFora adds nothing of its own to your document — no watermark, no branding. The output contains your pages plus your numbers, nothing else.

Can I number very large PDFs?

Yes. There is no page limit imposed by the tool. Since everything runs locally, a several-hundred-page file processes as fast as your own device allows, with no upload time at all.
