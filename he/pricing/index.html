שאלות נפוצות

Is it really free?

Yes. All 40+ browser-based tools are free with no sign-up and no watermark, forever.

Why is there a Pro plan?

A few tools (converting to/from Word, Excel and PowerPoint) need powerful servers. Pro will cover that cost. Everything else stays free.

Are my files private?

The free tools run entirely inside your browser — your files are never uploaded.
