Contact Us

Found a bug? Missing a tool you need? Something confusing? Tell us — real people read these messages, and feedback directly shapes what gets built next.

What to include

  • Bug reports: which tool, what you expected, what happened instead, and your browser if you know it.
  • Feature requests: describe the job you're trying to get done — we'll figure out the tool.
  • Privacy or legal questions: see our Privacy Policy and Terms first; if the answer isn't there, ask away.

We aim to reply within a couple of working days.
